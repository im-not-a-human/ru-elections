Gennady Zyuganov awarded title Hero of Labour of the Russian Federation

Vladimir Putin signed Executive Order On Awarding the Title Hero of Labour of the Russian Federation to Gennady Zyuganov.

June 26, 2024
12:00

The Presidential Executive Order awards the title of Hero of Labour of the Russian Federation to Gennady Zyuganov, a State Duma deputy, a member of the State Duma Committee on CIS Affairs, Eurasian Integration and Ties with Compatriots, and Head of the State Duma parliamentary faction of the Communist Party of the Russian Federation, for his outstanding contribution to the development of Russian statehood, civil society, and his long-time fruitful work.
