A · Origins

Founding congress 1 March 2020; Ministry of Justice registration within 3 weeks

On 1 March 2020, at the SAP Digital Leadership Centre in Moscow, the founding congress was held (120 delegates from 55 regions). On 24 March 2020 the Ministry of Justice issued the registration certificate — within 3 weeks. OGRN 1207700135972, INN 9706005582. For comparison: the Progress Party of Alexei Navalny (Алексей Навальный) was refused by the Ministry of Justice 8 times over 7 years (2011–2018). The registration came in the same 'window' as 'Za Pravdu' (For Truth) by Prilepin (25.03.2020) and Green Alternative (07.04.2020) — three new parties within six weeks before the 2021 Duma election cycle.

Three parties — New People, Za Pravdu, and Green Alternative — were registered by the Ministry of Justice within the same spring-2020 window. Click a party marker for details.

Founding timeline and registration details

The New People party was founded on 1 March 2020 at the SAP Digital Leadership Centre in Moscow — a major business forum under the brand of the software giant SAP. The congress was attended by 120 delegates from 55 regional branches. Legal identifiers: OGRN 1207700135972, INN 9706005582. The Ministry of Justice issued the registration certificate on 24 March 2020 — three weeks after the founding congress (a record pace for a non-parliamentary party).

The spring-2020 party wave

The founding coincided with a general 'party wave of 2020': alongside New People, the Ministry of Justice simultaneously registered 'Za Pravdu' (За правду) by Zakhar Prilepin (25.03.2020, founding congress 1.02.2020) and Green Alternative (Зелёная альтернатива) by Ruslan Khvostov (7.04.2020, founding congress 10.03.2020). Three projects acquired political-party status within six weeks before the 2021 Duma election cycle.

Electoral trajectory

The party had no electoral track record before the 8th Duma: it had not participated in federal elections independently before 2021. At the 2021 Duma election (17–19 September 2021) New People received 5.32% (2,991,130 votes) and formed a faction of 13 party-list deputies plus 2 single-member constituency deputies (G. Leonov, D. Pevtsov) = 15 seats in the 8th Duma.

Leaders and Presidential Administration ties

The party leader is Alexei Gennadyevich Nechayev (Алексей Геннадьевич Нечаев), founder of the MLM company Faberlic (cosmetics, direct sales, founded 1997). At the time New People was founded, Nechayev was already an authorised representative of the President of Russia in the 2018 elections (Presidential Decree No. 446-rp of 28.12.2017) and a member of the ONF Central Headquarters, 2019–2020.

The deputy chairman is Vladislav Alexandrovich Davankov (Владислав Александрович Даванков) (b. 1984). Before joining the party (2018–2021) he was deputy director-general of ANO Russia — Land of Opportunity (АНО «Россия — страна возможностей»), whose supervisory board is chaired by Sergei Kirienko, First Deputy Head of the Presidential Administration.

Comparative registration dynamics

Comparative registration dynamics:

  • New People — founding congress 01.03.2020, registration 24.03.2020. Time taken: 23 days.
  • Progress Party (Alexei Navalny) — registration attempts 2011–2018, 8 successive refusals from the Ministry of Justice. The party was never registered.
  • PARNAS — protracted procedure with suspensions. The core registration operation took approximately 18 months.

B · Financing

0% in 2021 → 92% in 2022 → 93% in 2023

In its first year (2021) the party was not entitled to state budget financing — ≈12 million roubles in donation income. After passing the 5% threshold at the 2021 Duma election (5.32%), the party became entitled to 152 roubles per vote under Art. 33 of Federal Law No. 95-FZ 'On Political Parties' — ≈455 million roubles/year. By 2023 the budget share reached 93% of all revenues.

Share of revenues from the federal budget (152 roubles/vote under Art. 33 of FZ-95). In two years: from 0% to 93%. Click a year pill for details.

The 152-rouble-per-vote formula

Under Art. 33 of Federal Law No. 95-FZ, parties that receive 3% or more in Duma elections receive annual payments from the federal budget at the rate of 152 roubles × N, where N is the number of votes cast for the party at the most recent Duma election. For New People: 2,991,130 × 152 roubles ≈ 455 million roubles/year until the next Duma election (scheduled for September 2026).

Revenue breakdown by year (million roubles):

  • 2021: ≈12 million roubles (donations only). Budget share — 0%.
  • 2022: ≈488 million roubles (455 million state budget + ≈33 million donations). Budget share — 92%.
  • 2023: ≈720 million roubles (≈669 million state budget + ≈51 million donations). Budget share — 93% (peak).
  • 2024: ≈619 million roubles (including a portion for Davankov's presidential campaign). Budget share — ≈90%.

Donation structuring

Largest donations in 2021 — alumni of Nechayev's 'Kapitany' ('Captains') programme. According to an investigation by Meduza × Transparency International (15.11.2022) a significant portion of donors were students and alumni of Nechayev's 'Kapitany' programme, interpreted as structuring a large donation through a large number of individuals (the per-individual cap being ≈4.33 million roubles).

Faberlic → Ministry of Defence

In April 2025, OOO 'Fesh Faktori' (Fashion Factory, a Faberlic sewing plant) was sold to OOO 'Voentekstilprom' — an entity affiliated with AO 'Voentorg' (Ministry of Defence of the Russian Federation). This is the first documented business-to-government asset transfer from the Nechayev group to the military circuit.

Presidential campaign 2024

Davankov's 2024 presidential campaign. Campaign fund — limit 400 million roubles; actually spent ≈130 million roubles; result — 3.85% (≈3.4 million votes), 3rd place. According to Forbes (March 2024) the party's net profit from the presidential campaign was ≈150 million roubles — between LDPR (≈220 million) and CPRF (≈407 million).

C · Leaders

Six people for 15 seats: founder + deputy + 4 public figures

The leader is Alexei Nechayev, founder of Faberlic, Putin's authorised representative in 2018, ONF member. The deputy leader is Vladislav Davankov, alumnus of ANO 'RSV' under Kirienko (Presidential Administration), presidential candidate in 2024. In the Duma: Sardana Avksentyeva (Сардана Авксентьева, former mayor of Yakutsk), single-member constituency deputies G. Leonov and D. Pevtsov (actor). The party is weakly personalised beyond the Nechayev–Davankov pair; the regional network and Party Council consist of names without public biographies.

Structural pattern

Structural pattern: the party presents itself as 'a new generation' and 'liberal technocrats', but its first and second in command are established figures in public communication with the state apparatus. For Nechayev, the key institutional path runs from MLM entrepreneur through ONF-2019 to authorised representative in 2018. For Davankov — from ANO 'RSV' (under Kirienko) to deputy chair of New People. The Nechayev–Davankov pairing accounts for ≥85% of the party's public presence; the remaining 4–5 leaders appear rarely outside the Duma framework.

D · State ties

Authorised representative 2018, ONF, Faberlic → Ministry of Defence 2025

A. Nechayev — one of ≈500 authorised representatives of Putin in 2018 (Decree No. 446-rp of 28.12.2017), member of the ONF Central Headquarters 2019–2020. In April 2025, OOO 'Fesh Faktori' (Faberlic's sewing plant) was sold to a structure affiliated with AO 'Voentorg' of the Russian Ministry of Defence. No current party leadership members hold direct state positions — the ties are indirect but persistent.

Authorised representative 2018

A. Nechayev — authorised representative of the President of Russia in the 2018 elections. Decree No. 446-rp of 28.12.2017 (text). The list included ≈500 persons — public figures expressing support for the candidate. This status legally expires after the election, but politically anchors Nechayev within the presidential circuit until the founding of New People in 2020.

ONF Central Headquarters member 2019–2020

Member of the ONF Central Headquarters (Центральный штаб ОНФ) 2019–2020. The ONF (All-Russia People's Front, Общероссийский народный фронт) is a civic organisation founded in 2011 on the President's initiative — effectively an umbrella for pro-government organisations. Nechayev's membership in the Central Headquarters immediately before founding New People is a public signal that the party project was cleared within the presidential vertical.

ANO 'RSV' — Davankov

V. Davankov — deputy director-general of ANO 'RSV', 2018–2021. ANO Russia — Land of Opportunity (АНО «Россия — страна возможностей») is a non-profit organisation under the supervisory board of Sergei Kirienko (First Deputy Head of the Presidential Administration). It runs the competitions 'Leaders of Russia', 'Bolshaya Peremena' and others. RSV is the Presidential Administration's primary personnel incubator. Davankov's position was an operational-management role, not a formal government post.

Faberlic → Ministry of Defence 2025

Faberlic → Voentekstilprom → AO 'Voentorg' (Ministry of Defence), April 2025. OOO 'Fesh Faktori' — a sewing plant belonging to the Faberlic group of A. Nechayev. According to TAdviser, in April 2025 the plant was sold to OOO 'Voentekstilprom', affiliated with AO 'Voentorg' — a structure of the Russian Ministry of Defence. The exact deal price has not been disclosed. This is the first documented business-to-government asset transfer from the Nechayev business perimeter to the defence circuit after the start of Davankov's presidential campaign.

E · Voting

26 'in favour' out of 28 restrictive laws 2022–2025

Across 28 key restrictive votes in the 8th Duma, the New People faction voted in favour 26 times, partially against once, and against as a bloc once. The only systemic 'against' vote was Federal Law No. 281-FZ of 22.07.2025 (the VPN/extremism-search law): 0 in favour / 13 against / 0 abstained / 2 did not vote. On mobilisation (20.09.2022) — the faction voted in favour, and V. Davankov was co-author of the second-reading amendments.

Methodology

Methodology: 28 votes from 2022–2025 were selected from the categories 'military-repressive', 'foreign-agent', 'gender-conservative', 'digital control', and 'budget/social policy'. Raw data sources: research/compromat/05-evidence/duma-api/votes/ (raw XML from api.duma.gov.ru), sozd.duma.gov.ru. Results 'in favour / against / abstained / did not vote' — for the New People faction (15 deputies in the 8th Duma).

The only systemic 'against' — FZ-281 (VPN)

The only faction-level 'against' vote in 2022–2025 — 22 July 2025, Federal Law No. 281-FZ (expansion of VPN blocking powers + introduction of liability for 'searching for extremist materials', even without downloading or viewing). Third reading: 306 in favour, 67 against, 22 abstained. New People faction — 13 against, 0 in favour, 2 did not vote. This is the only behavioural break with the pro-government line across the entire convocation (beyond some budget votes, where New People were partial abstainers).

Davankov's co-authorship of mobilisation

20 September 2022, Federal Law No. 365-FZ (mobilisation amendments to the Criminal Code — Articles 207.3, 280.3, 352.1, 356.1). Third reading — vote_id 119076 in the API: 389 in favour, 0 against, 0 abstained, 61 did not vote. New People faction — 13 in favour, 0 against, 0 abstained, 2 did not vote. Davankov personally — one of the co-authors of the second-reading amendments per Kommersant, Rossiyskaya Gazeta and 72.ru. Eighteen months later, that same Davankov stood in the 2024 presidential election on the slogan 'peace and negotiations with Ukraine on our terms'.

F · Managed opposition

The most pristine 'new' party — with a solid Presidential-Administration biography at the top

New People satisfies every structural hallmark of 'managed opposition': budget dependency (≈90–93%), registration speed (3 weeks against 7 years / 8 refusals for Navalny's Progress Party), leadership biographies (Nechayev — Putin's authorised representative 2018; Davankov — alumnus of ANO 'RSV' under Kirienko), voting record (92–93% in favour of restrictions), plus absorption of liquidated projects (Titov's Party of Growth merged into New People in April 2024). Yet the party presents itself as 'a new generation of technocrats'.

Comparison with the other 'new' parties of spring 2020

Comparison with the other 'new' parties of spring 2020:

  • 'Za Pravdu' by Prilepin (1.02.2020) — registered by the Ministry of Justice 25.03.2020. In September 2021 merged with A Just Russia to form A Just Russia — For Truth (SRZP; Кириенко publicly participated in coordinating the merger; see SRZP dossier). Prilepin before the party — a well-known public figure with state awards.
  • Green Alternative (10.03.2020) — registered 7.04.2020. 2021 Duma election: 0.64%. Weakly personalised; its systemic function is to siphon votes in the 'green' niche; the party is not in the Duma.

All three parties — New People, 'Za Pravdu', and Green Alternative — were founded and registered within six weeks before the 2021 Duma election cycle. Of them, only one (New People) entered the Duma. This was not accidental: it was the only one that managed to organise mass financing and a media presence in the pre-campaign phase. The source of that scale was structured donations from individuals (Meduza × TI), plus corporate resources from the Nechayev group (Faberlic, the 'Kapitany' programme).

Merger with the Party of Growth

Merger with B. Titov's Party of Growth (19 April 2024). The Party of Growth, which did not enter the 8th Duma (≈1% in the 2021 Duma election), merged into New People. Party of Growth personnel transferred to the New People Council; the Party of Growth's legal entity was liquidated by the Supreme Court of Russia on 20.11.2025. See the Party of Growth dossier.

G · Crises and war

The Davankov paradox: 18 months between mobilisation and 'peace'

20 September 2022. V. Davankov — co-author of the second-reading mobilisation amendments (Articles 207.3, 280.3, 352.1, 356.1 of the Criminal Code). 18 months later, that same Davankov stood in the 2024 presidential election on the slogan 'peace and negotiations with Ukraine on our terms' — 3.85%, 3rd place. This is a structural story, not a contradiction in position: the party simultaneously provides the legislative framework for the war and a chance for 'a liberal voice', keeping a zone of systemic permissibility.

February–March 2022 — the start

February–March 2022. No anti-war statement from the party. 4 March 2022 — vote in favour of FZ-32 on 'false information about the army': New People faction — 13 in favour, 0 against, 0 abstained, 2 did not vote.

20 September 2022 — mobilisation

20 September 2022 — mobilisation. Federal Law No. 365-FZ. Third reading in a single plenary day. New People faction — unanimously in favour. V. Davankov — co-author of the second-reading amendments on which the third reading was based. This is not 'the faction's voice' — it is authorship, i.e. participation in drafting the criminal norms on desertion, 'voluntary surrender', and evasion of mobilisation registration.

3 October 2022 — annexation of 4 regions

3 October 2022 — ratification of the accession of 4 regions. New People faction — unanimously in favour. No individual 'against' votes.

22–23 December 2023 — nomination as candidate

22–23 December 2023. The 11th congress of New People nominates Davankov as a candidate for President of Russia. Campaign programme — 'social-liberal, technocratic', 'reducing bureaucratic burden on business', 'a new young Russia'.

Two lanes: DUMA (co-authorship of mobilisation, annexation, confiscation) and CAMPAIGN (nomination, 'peace and negotiations', 'Migrant ID'). A red dashed arrow connects 20.09.2022 and 17.03.2024.

January–February 2024 — the 'peace' campaign

January–February 2024. The campaign develops the rhetoric of 'peace and negotiations with Ukraine on our terms'. This is the first and only public candidate from a parliamentary party with openly de-escalatory rhetoric in the 2024 presidential election.

17 March 2024 — result

17 March 2024. Result: 3,850,027 votes, 3.85%, 3rd place (after Putin 87.28% and Kharitonov of CPRF 4.31%). Slutsky of LDPR — 4th place with 3.20%. Davankov outpolled Slutsky, which is itself an indicator of the existence of a latent 'anti-war' vote within the system.

22 March 2024 — Crocus + 'Migrant ID'

22 March 2024 — Crocus City Hall terrorist attack. Five days after the election. That same week, A. Nechayev announced the 'Migrant ID' initiative — mandatory digital tracking of labour migrants. This represents a pivot towards the right-conservative flank.

Structural meaning

The structural meaning: the party simultaneously provides the legislative framework for the war through Davankov's co-authorship of the mobilisation amendments, and simultaneously provides a channel for 'the liberal voice' in the presidential election through that same Davankov's negotiation rhetoric. This is not a contradiction but a dual function: framework for the war and a managed-release valve for 'liberal discontent' in a direction safe for the system.
